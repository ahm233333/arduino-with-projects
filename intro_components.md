## 7. My Recommended Learning Sequence

Follow this sequence rather than trying to learn everything simultaneously.

| Phase | Learning Area | Suggested Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Electronics | 2 weeks |
| 2 | Circuit Design and Breadboarding | 1 week |
| 3 | Arduino Programming | 3 weeks |
| 4 | Sensors and Actuators | 2 weeks |
| 5 | Intermediate Electronics | 2 weeks |
| 6 | ESP32 and IoT | 2 weeks |
| 7 | PLC Fundamentals | 2 weeks |
| 8 | PLC Ladder Logic | 3 weeks |
| 9 | Industrial Automation | 2 weeks |
| 10 | Integrated Projects | 3 weeks |

**Total: Approximately 21 weeks.**

### Important advice

Don't just memorise component names. For every component, learn these six things:

1. What is it?
2. How does it work?
3. What is its electrical specification?
4. How do you connect it?
5. How do you program or control it?
6. Where is it used in real life?

Your ultimate target should be to build systems that combine **electronics + embedded programming + sensors + actuators + industrial automation**.
